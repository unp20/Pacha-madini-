About

The Journey of Pacha Madini

Pacha Madini (Hassan Athumani) is a Rapper, Songwriter and Hip-Hop Artist from Tanzania.

He started music in 2014, and has released 8 songs including 'On My Way', 'Voice ya Pacha', 'Umeniweza', 'Shabiki', 'MWAKE' and 'BINGWA'.
